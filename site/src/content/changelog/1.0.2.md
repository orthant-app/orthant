- Shortcut labels follow your keyboard layout. On Dvorak, Canadian, AZERTY or a Cyrillic layout, the key shown in Settings is the key printed on your keyboard, and it updates the moment you switch input source. Existing shortcuts keep firing from the same physical keys.
- Shortcuts can now use punctuation, function keys, Home, End, Page Up, Page Down, Forward Delete and the numeric keypad.
- The grid works with VoiceOver: opening it announces the captured window and the grid size, each move announces the selected cells and target size, and Move, Extend, Place window and Cancel are exposed as actions.
- The update feed links each earlier release to its own download again.
